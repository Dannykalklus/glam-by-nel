Keep Them Gorgeous

Prep & aftercare

  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes: no mascara or eyeliner.
  • Remove contact lenses before your session.
  • Skip oily creams and serums around the eyes on the day.
  • Wear something comfy. You'll be lying back with your eyes closed, so treat it as a nap.
  • Keep your lashes dry. Avoid steam, saunas and very hot showers.
  • Don't rub, pull or pick at your new set.
  • Try to sleep on your back, or on a silk pillowcase.
  • Cleanse gently with an oil-free lash cleanser a few times a week.
  • Brush through with a clean spoolie every morning.
  • Avoid oil-based removers, waterproof mascara and lash curlers.
  • Natural lashes shed every day, so book an infill every 2–3 weeks to stay full.
  • Come to infills with clean lashes so the new extensions bond well.
  • Questions about your set? Message Nel on WhatsApp anytime.
